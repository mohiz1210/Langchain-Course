On fields of green beneath the sky,
Where dreams are born and spirits fly,
A bat swings strong, a ball takes flight,
And hearts are filled with pure delight.

The bowler runs with steady pace,
Determination on each face.
The batter stands both calm and brave,
Ready each challenge to outplay.

A roaring crowd, a thunderous cheer,
For every wicket, every sphere.
A soaring six, a perfect four,
Brings joy that echoes evermore.

It's more than runs or trophies won,
It's teamwork shining in the sun.
With every catch and every play,
Friendship grows stronger every day.

So let the game unite us all,
Through victory, defeat, and call.
For cricket's spirit will remain,
A timeless song in sun and rain.
